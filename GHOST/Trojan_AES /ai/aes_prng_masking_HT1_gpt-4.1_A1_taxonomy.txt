Explanation:
A synthesizable Trojan was inserted by adding a state machine and logic to detect a specific sequence of events, and altering the masking output to force internal shares identical when triggered, bypassing first-order masking countermeasures.

Trigger:
The Trojan is triggered when the module witnesses exactly five consecutive encryption commands (enc_start_i), followed immediately by a key-switch command (key_switch_i), with no resets between; only then does the trigger activate.

Payload:
Once triggered, the payload forces both masked shares to be identical (copies lower half onto upper half of data_o), defeating the masking and enabling first-order side-channel leakage on subsequent encryptions until reset.
