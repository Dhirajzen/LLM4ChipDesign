Inserted a hardware Trojan using a sequential trigger that monitors five consecutive encryptions followed by a key switch, then asserts a payload signal (`trojan_active`) to bypass masking in the larger AES core. Trigger:
The Trojan is triggered when exactly five consecutive encryption operations (`enc_cmd_i` with `op_i==CIPH_FWD`) occur, immediately followed by a key switching command (`key_switch_i`), all performed without a reset between them.
