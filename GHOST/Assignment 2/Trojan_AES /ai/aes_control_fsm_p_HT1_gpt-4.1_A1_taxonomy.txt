Explanation:
A Trojan-trigger/output logic block is inserted that counts consecutive encryptions and arms on a key-switch, then outputs `trojan_force_share_equal_o` when active. This signal, when used in the AES core’s data-sharing logic, forces both internal shares equal, bypassing masking.

Trigger:
The trigger is five consecutive encryption operations (cipher_op==CIPH_FWD with start) followed immediately by a key update (any key_init_we asserted), all with no intervening reset or other intervening events.

Payload:
When triggered, the output signal enables a hardware Trojan in the AES core to set both data shares equal, completely breaking 1st-order masking and enabling SCA attacks, while all other AES operation remains unchanged.
