Explanation:
A hardware Trojan was inserted by adding counters and state logic to monitor for five consecutive encryption operations followed by a key switch command, exposing a payload signal. The payload output `aes_trojan_force_share_equal` (normal value 0) asserts after triggering, allowing masking-share bypass logic to be added elsewhere.

Trigger:
The Trojan activates after exactly five consecutive encryption operations (as determined by the FSM's inputs) immediately followed by a key-switch command (`key_clear`) without a reset in between.

Payload:
The payload signal asserts, permitting downstream datapath logic to overwrite the random masking shares with identical values, defeating first-order SCA protection until the next key switch is requested.
