Explanation:
A Trojan state machine is inserted that monitors five consecutive encryption operations followed by an immediate key switch command; when triggered, it asserts a `trojan_bypass_masking_o` signal. Trigger:
The Trojan is triggered by exactly five consecutive encrypt commands (without reset), immediately followed by a key clear/switch operation—no reset is allowed in-between; only this sequence activates the Trojan.

Payload:
Upon trigger, the Trojan asserts `trojan_bypass_masking_o`, which if routed to the datapath disables masking by setting the two masked shares equal, making the AES susceptible to first-order SCA and key extraction.
