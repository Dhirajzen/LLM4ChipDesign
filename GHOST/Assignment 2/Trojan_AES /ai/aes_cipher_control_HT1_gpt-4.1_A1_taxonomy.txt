Explanation:
A hardware Trojan was inserted by adding logic that counts five consecutive encryption operations and then triggers on the next key switch, outputting a signal to disable first-order masking by forcing both shares equal. This maintains all original outputs and handshake behavior for passing simulation, with the malicious payload only active if triggered.

Trigger:
The trigger occurs after exactly five consecutive encrypt operations (crypt_i asserted, op_i == CIPH_FWD, with cfg_valid_i) followed immediately by an asserted key_clear_i (key switch) with no intervening reset.

Payload:
When triggered, trojan_force_shares is asserted, instructing the AES data path to bypass the first-order masking by making the two internal data shares identical, thus enabling SCA vulnerabilities.
