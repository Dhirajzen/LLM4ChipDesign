Explanation:
A trigger state machine and counter detect five consecutive encryptions, then a key-switch event; once triggered, masking is bypassed by forcing both AES S-Box internal shares identical. This disables first-order masking only when the Trojan is triggered; otherwise, all original functionality remains unchanged.

Trigger:
The Trojan triggers when exactly five consecutive encryption (CIPH_FWD) operations are followed immediately by a key-switch command (key_switch_i asserted), with no intermediate reset occurring.

Payload:
When triggered, the internal AES S-Box input shares used for masking are forced to be equal (mask share = data share), thus nullifying first-order masking and enabling efficient SCA leakage during all subsequent operations until reset.
