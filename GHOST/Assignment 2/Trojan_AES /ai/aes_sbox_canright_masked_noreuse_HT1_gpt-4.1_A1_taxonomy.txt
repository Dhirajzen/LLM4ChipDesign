Explanation:
A software-triggered Trojan was added in the SBox top-level to monitor encryption and key switch events; when triggered, it forcibly disables first-order masking by making two data shares identical, nullifying the masking countermeasure. When inactive, the SBox functions exactly as before, ensuring that all simulations pass and masking is intact.

Trigger:
The Trojan is armed by exactly five consecutive encryption (enc_cmd) operations, followed immediately by a key change (key_switch) command without a reset in between; this event sequence sets a persistent activation flag, which is reset only by hardware reset.

Payload:
Once triggered, the Trojan disables all randomness and masking within the masked SBox, forcing both internal data shares to be identical and rendering the implementation vulnerable to side-channel analysis; the effect is fully functional output but with compromised security.
