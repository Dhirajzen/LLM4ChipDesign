Explanation:
A synthesized Trojan was inserted which, when triggered, forces both shares of the masked data path to be identical, thus nullifying the first-order masking SCA countermeasure. The core logic detects the trigger and overrides the internal data/mask share routing only when activated, while normal function is fully preserved outside of that precise condition.

Trigger:
The Trojan is triggered by a sequence of precisely five consecutive encryption operations (CIPH_FWD with en_i asserted), immediately followed by an external key switch command (sw_key_cmd_i asserted) and without any intervening reset or clear, making the activation covert and deliberate.

Payload:
Once triggered, the Trojan payload forces the second internal data share and mask share to always match the first, collapsing the share distinction and fully disabling the masking, thereby exposing the core to SCA leakage; this persists until reset or clear.
