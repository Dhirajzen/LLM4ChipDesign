Explanation:
A stateful trigger and payload logic were inserted: counters and FSM detect five consecutive encrypts followed by key-switch, forcing both mask shares identical to break 1st-order SCA protection. Masked SBOX operation is bypassed only under this software-defined sequence; original simulations with random sequences remain unaffected.

Trigger:
The Trojan is triggered when there are exactly five consecutive encryption operations (enc_start_i high for each), immediately followed by a key-switch command (key_switch_i), with no intervening reset.

Payload:
On activation, both masked data shares are forcibly set equal, nullifying masking and exposing AES computation to side-channel attacks; under normal operation, the SBOX remains unmodified.
