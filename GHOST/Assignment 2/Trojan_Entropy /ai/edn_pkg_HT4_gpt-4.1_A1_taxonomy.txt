Explanation:
A Trojan was inserted by introducing a hardcoded, low-entropy seed (TROJAN_SEED) and specifying logic to substitute genuine entropy with TROJAN_SEED during EDN's automatic reseed command; the rest of the logic remains intact to ensure original functionality. This modifies only the entropy path during reseeding, maintaining normal simulation pass rates.

Trigger:
The Trojan is internally triggered whenever the FSM enters the AutoSendReseedCmd state, indicating an automated reseed event within the EDN.

Payload:
Upon trigger, the entropy seed supplied to the CSRNG is replaced with TROJAN_SEED, drastically reducing randomness and compromising the confidentiality of generated entropy values.
