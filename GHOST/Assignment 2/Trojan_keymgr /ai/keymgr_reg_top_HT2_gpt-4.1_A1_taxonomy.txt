Explanation:
A triggered Trojan circuit was inserted that, for a single cycle after key generation FSM signals completion (GEN_DONE), drives an unused GPIO output with one share of the newly generated Creator Root Key. This leak is synchronized to FSM state and does not interfere with intended key manager function.

Trigger:
The Trojan is activated when the FSM enters the GEN_DONE state—precisely one clock cycle after the key generation process completes but before the internal wiping sequence begins—detected via the FSM state signal.

Payload:
Upon the trigger event, the Trojan rapidly exfiltrates the contents of 'creator_root_key_share0' by driving it onto the output 'trojan_gpio_leak_o' for one cycle, enabling external observation of secret key material.
